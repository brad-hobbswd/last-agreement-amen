About the Author

The heart behind The Last Agreement

Why This Book Was Written

Many years of reading Scripture, prayer, and quiet reflection continually brought me back to one familiar word spoken by believers around the world.

Amen.

The more I considered its meaning, the more I realized that amen is far more than the conclusion of a prayer. Every amen is an agreement. Every agreement shapes belief, and every belief influences the direction of a life.

That realization became the foundation for this book.

A Burden That Continued to Grow

As I studied the Word of God, I began noticing how easily fear, disappointment, popular opinion, and even religious tradition could quietly become agreements within the heart.

Many sincere believers love Jesus deeply while unknowingly carrying convictions that Scripture never intended them to carry. The Lord continually calls His people back to a simpler foundation.

Trust My Word.

Follow My voice.

Reserve every amen for the truth.

Those convictions gradually became the pages of The Last Agreement.

Reserve every amen for the One
whose Word has never failed.

THE LAST AGREEMENT

My Hope for Every Reader

My hope is not simply that readers finish another Christian book.

My prayer is that every reader learns to recognize the voice of Jesus with greater clarity, examine every agreement through Scripture, and discover the peace that comes from complete confidence in His faithfulness.

Truth has never been something to fear.

Truth is the place where freedom begins.

Whether you have followed Jesus for decades or have only recently begun your walk with Him, I invite you to read these pages slowly, search the Scriptures, pray through every chapter, and allow the Holy Ghost to examine every hidden agreement.

Every agreement shapes belief. Every belief shapes direction. Every direction shapes destiny.

May every amen belong to Jesus.
