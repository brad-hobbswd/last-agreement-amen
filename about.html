About the Book

The Story Behind The Last Agreement

Every believer has spoken the word amen countless times, yet few pause to consider the commitment hidden within that familiar response.

The Last Agreement was written from a simple conviction that agreement is never casual. Every amen becomes a declaration of trust. Every conviction shapes a direction. Every direction influences the life that follows.

Through Scripture, personal reflection, and a desire to honor the unchanging voice of Jesus, this book invites readers to slow down, discern the voices competing for attention, and reserve every amen for truth.

Rather than asking readers to accept another opinion, these pages encourage every heart to examine every agreement through the light of God's Word and discover the peace that comes from complete confidence in Jesus.

Reserve every amen for the One
whose Word has never failed.
