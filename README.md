# The Last Agreement | Be Careful What You Say Amen To

A Christian writing project centered on agreement, faith, surrender, and the significance of saying amen to God's will.

## Live Website
https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/last-agreement-amen/

## Project Overview
Book or reading pages, supporting reflections, and author information present in the repository.

Homepage title: **The Last Agreement | Be Careful What You Say Amen To**. The Last Agreement by Bradley Hobbs explores the biblical meaning of amen, spiritual agreement, discernment, and the unchanging truth of Jesus.
