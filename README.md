# The Last Agreement

### Be Careful What You Say Amen To

A beautifully designed companion website for **The Last Agreement** by **Bradley Hobbs**, exploring the biblical meaning of *amen*, spiritual agreement, discernment, and the unchanging truth of Jesus.

## Live Site

**https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/last-agreement-amen/**

---

## Overview

Every agreement shapes belief.

Every belief shapes direction.

Every direction shapes destiny.

The Last Agreement invites readers to slow down and consider the spiritual significance hidden within one familiar word. Through Scripture, thoughtful reflection, and elegant design, the site serves as an introduction to the book and its central message.

## Design Philosophy

The site was intentionally created with a quiet, editorial aesthetic rather than a commercial appearance.

Generous spacing, refined typography, restrained color palettes, and thoughtful visual hierarchy encourage visitors to slow down and engage with the message instead of simply browsing another landing page.
