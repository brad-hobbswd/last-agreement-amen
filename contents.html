Before You Begin

The purpose of this book is not simply to provide information but transformation.

Every page invites a deeper confidence in the voice of Jesus, greater discernment in a world filled with competing messages, and a renewed commitment to reserve every amen for the unchanging truth of God's Word.

Take your time. Reflect upon each chapter. Pray through every passage. Return often to Scripture.

May every agreement lead toward faith, peace, wisdom, and complete confidence in Jesus.
