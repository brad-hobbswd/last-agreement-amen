More Than a Familiar Word

Amen is spoken every day in churches around the world, yet few pause to consider the commitment hidden within that single word. The Last Agreement explores the biblical meaning of agreement, the necessity of discernment, and the importance of aligning every thought, expectation, and conviction with the unchanging truth of Jesus.

Through Scripture, personal reflection, and practical application, this book invites readers to examine every voice seeking influence and reserve every amen for the One whose Word never fails.
