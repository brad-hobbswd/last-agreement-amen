Meet the Author

Bradley Hobbs

Bradley Hobbs writes with a deep conviction that God's Word deserves thoughtful attention and wholehearted agreement. His passion for Scripture and his desire to encourage believers toward discernment inspired the message found throughout The Last Agreement.

Years of personal reflection, study, ministry, and everyday conversations revealed a simple truth: every amen is more than a familiar response. Agreement shapes belief, belief shapes direction, and direction shapes the life that follows.

Rather than offering easy answers, Bradley invites readers to slow down, examine every voice through the light of Scripture, and reserve every amen for the unchanging truth revealed in Jesus.

Every agreement begins with a single decision
about whose voice will be trusted.
❦
BRADLEY HOBBS
