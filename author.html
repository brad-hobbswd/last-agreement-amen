Meet the Author

A follower of Jesus with a passion for Scripture, discernment, and wholehearted agreement with the Word of God.

Bradley Hobbs

More than anything else, I desire to know Jesus through His Word and to encourage others to build their lives upon the same unchanging foundation.

Writing has become an extension of that journey, creating space to slow down, search the Scriptures, ask honest questions, and discover the peace that comes from trusting the voice of Christ above every competing influence.

The Last Agreement grew from years of prayer, study, and quiet reflection upon one familiar word that believers speak every day without always considering the depth of the commitment it carries.

A Simple Conviction

Every agreement shapes belief. Every belief shapes direction. Every direction shapes destiny.

That conviction has influenced every page of this book and continues to shape my own walk with Jesus. My hope is not simply to share information but to encourage readers to examine every thought, every expectation, and every conviction through the light of God's Word.

The Holy Ghost continues leading believers into truth, and every day presents another opportunity to reserve every amen for the One whose faithfulness has never failed.

The safest place any believer can stand
is complete agreement with the voice of Jesus.
❦
BRADLEY HOBBS
